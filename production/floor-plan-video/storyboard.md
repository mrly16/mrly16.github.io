# Spatial graph hero video

Target duration: 15 seconds  
Format: 16:9, 1920 x 1080, silent-first  
Loop: optional, with the graph resolving back into the raster drawing

## Shot 1 - A picture, not data

**Time:** 00:00-00:03  
**Asset:** `01-raster-plan.png`

Start on a close crop of the raster plan. Move slowly across dimensions,
furniture, labels, and thick wall pixels. Pull back just enough to reveal the
complete plan.

On-screen copy:

- EN: `A floor plan begins as pixels.`
- ZH: `一张户型图，最初只是像素。`

## Shot 2 - Geometry emerges

**Time:** 00:03-00:06  
**Assets:** `02-wall-contours.png`,
`overlays/wall-contours-transparent.png`

Do not crossfade to the contour image. Trace the exterior boundary first, then
internal walls. Let small irregularities remain for half a second so the next
optimization has a visible purpose.

On-screen copy:

- EN: `Boundaries become geometry.`
- ZH: `边界被还原为几何。`

## Shot 3 - Relationships become explicit

**Time:** 00:06-00:10  
**Assets:** `03-spatial-graph.png`,
`overlays/spatial-graph-transparent.png`

Introduce nodes at corners, then draw connecting edges. Slightly displace a few
nodes before snapping them into horizontal and vertical alignment. This is the
main visual event.

On-screen copy:

- EN: `Geometry becomes a spatial graph.`
- ZH: `几何进一步成为空间图。`

## Shot 4 - Structure returns to the source

**Time:** 00:10-00:13  
**Asset:** `04-graph-on-plan.jpg`

Bring the original raster back beneath the graph. Keep the graph visible long
enough to demonstrate correspondence between the image and the structured
representation.

On-screen copy:

- EN: `Every node and edge carries meaning.`
- ZH: `每个节点与连接都有明确含义。`

## Shot 5 - Production evidence

**Time:** 00:13-00:15  
**Assets:** `05-source-to-segmentation.webp` or
`06-semantic-output.jpg`

Cut to a separate production example. Treat it as a new sample rather than the
same plan. Finish with one evidence statement.

Primary copy:

- EN: `70K floor plans reconstructed`
- ZH: `完成 7 万张户型图重建`

Secondary copy:

- EN: `Raster input to editable spatial data`
- ZH: `从栅格图像到可编辑空间数据`

## Motion system

- Trace boundaries at constant speed with slight acceleration near corners.
- Give graph nodes a short 120-180 ms locking motion when constraints resolve.
- Use hard geometric masks and restrained camera movement.
- Keep transitions between 350 and 600 ms.
- Use one warm line color and one cool node color.
- Avoid continuous floating motion after a stage has resolved.

## Sound direction

The video must remain understandable without sound. Optional sound design:

- soft paper or scanner texture for the raster stage;
- dry line-drawing ticks for contour extraction;
- subtle mechanical clicks as nodes lock into alignment;
- one low, resolved tone when the structured model is complete.
